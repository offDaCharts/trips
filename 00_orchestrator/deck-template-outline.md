# Trip Slide Deck Template

Use this outline for one Google Slides deck per trip.

1. Title: destination, dates, hero image
2. Why this trip fits us
3. The route at a glance
4. Vibe board: landscapes, stays, food, baby experience
5. Recommended itinerary by day
6. Stay options: practical base, special stay, splurge
7. Anchor activities and baby-friendly notes
8. Budget estimate
9. Tradeoffs and risks
10. Final recommendation

Deck style:

- image-forward, not text-heavy
- include real photos or sourced/generative destination imagery
- include a simple route map or route diagram
- show costs in one clean table
- keep baby logistics visible but not dominant
