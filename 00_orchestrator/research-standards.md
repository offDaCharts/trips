# Trip Research Standards

Planning window: around September 3-16, 2026, flexible by one or two days.

Travelers: two adults and one baby who will have just turned one.

Budget target: around or just under $10,000, with selective one-night or two-night splurges acceptable. Avoid lodging above about $900/night unless it is a deliberate short splurge.

Preferred vibe:

- mountain scenery, easy-to-moderate hiking, lakes, farms, animals, and village life
- farm-to-table food and farmstead stays when possible
- wellness/spa moments mixed into an active outdoor trip
- baby-friendly pacing, short travel days, places to crawl/explore, and backup bad-weather activities

Research rules:

- Use current sources for flight/stay availability and pricing.
- Favor official hotel, tourism, national park, airline, or booking pages when possible.
- Capture links for every stay, activity, and claim that may influence a decision.
- Track whether an activity is stroller-friendly, carrier-friendly, or probably not worth it with a one-year-old.
- Identify splurge nights separately from practical base nights.
