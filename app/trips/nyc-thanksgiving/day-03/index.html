DAY 03 · THURSDAY · NOVEMBER 26

The balloons are here.

The 100th Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade, then a long lunch and a very good nap.

← Back to the five-day plan

A REALISTIC RHYTHM

The day, hour by hour.

07:00–08:15

Breakfast with a view

For a confirmed view-room: settle by the window with breakfast. For public viewing: a much earlier ~6 a.m. arrival is a planning target, subject to the official 2026 access plan.

08:30 onward

Parade morning

Macy’s confirms the Nov 26 broadcast starts at 8:30 a.m. Local passing time varies by block; do not assume balloons reach your hotel at 8:30. Ask the hotel for its viewing window.

12:00–14:30

Quiet room time + nap

Let street crowds clear. Avoid committing to a cross-route car pickup immediately after the parade.

15:00–16:30

Thanksgiving at the table

First choice: a confirmed hotel meal or Jams if its holiday menu fits. Tavern on the Green is the romantic alternative; 2026 menu and availability need checking.

16:30–18:00

A short post-feast walk

Take a simple loop on your side of the parade route once police reopen access. Then back for a cozy evening; AMNH is closed today.

At the table

$320 family planning allowance with tax/tip: breakfast and light snacks ~$70, Thanksgiving meal ~$250. This is not a quoted menu. A premium prix fixe can exceed it; ask about Rue’s charge and high chair.

With Rue

Warm room, crib and a bathroom are the strongest argument for paying for a view. For street viewing, use layers, carrier and hearing protection if needed; do not depend on reliable toilet access or being able to re-enter a viewing pen.

How to get around

Walk today. Road closures can block taxis, hotel entrances and cross-avenue access. Request the hotel’s guest access instructions and avoid an airport transfer this morning.

If the weather or mood turns

Bad weather or a tired child: watch the broadcast in the room, go out briefly only if comfortable, and keep the meal as the day’s second highlight. Strong winds can change balloon operations.

Before you go

Secure the precise view category and meal reservation. Public curbside viewing is free; an event ticket buys a private venue service, not a general Macy’s grandstand ticket.
