DAY 02 · WEDNESDAY · NOVEMBER 25

Big wonders, little moments.

Dinosaurs, a warm lunch, and a possible first look at the giant balloons.

A REALISTIC RHYTHM

The day, hour by hour.

08:00

Breakfast near the hotel

Leave the big stroller bag behind; carry only museum and weather essentials.

10:00–11:45

American Museum of Natural History

Book general admission. Pick dinosaurs and the blue whale rather than trying to cover every hall. Museum hours are 10 a.m.–5:30 p.m.; closed tomorrow.

12:00–14:30

Lunch + protected nap

Eat on the Upper West Side, then return to the hotel for a proper nap. If stroller naps are working, use a quiet park loop instead.

15:00–16:30

Optional balloon inflation

Traditionally held around AMNH on Thanksgiving Eve. The 2026 hours, entrance and security route are not verified; replace this block if the official announcement does not fit. Budget for queues, not a quick walk-through.

17:00

Early supper + tomorrow’s setup

Prepare breakfast, warm layers and a thermos. Confirm hotel access instructions for the parade and keep the morning flexible.

At the table

$180 family allowance: bagel breakfast ~$30, Upper West Side lunch ~$55, casual supper ~$75, snacks ~$20. Pick a nearby café over crossing Midtown at rush hour.

With Rue

AMNH has elevators and stroller access; carry Rue when crowds get dense. Inflation is a carrier-first optional outing, not an obligation. A two-hour queue is a good reason to skip it. Hotel nap and floor play are protected.

How to get around

Hotel → AMNH: roughly 20–35 minutes by transit or 30–45 minutes on foot from northern Midtown. Check elevator status; allow extra time around event closures.

If the weather or mood turns

Stay longer at AMNH or add the Butterfly Vivarium with a timed upgrade. If inflation is crowded, let the parade itself be the reveal and enjoy a calm dinner.

Before you go

Reserve the museum date and optional exhibition. Recheck Macy’s inflation announcement a week before travel and again on Wednesday.
