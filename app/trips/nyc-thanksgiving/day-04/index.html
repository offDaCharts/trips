A REALISTIC RHYTHM

The day, hour by hour.

09:00–10:30

Rockefeller Center + Fifth Avenue

See the architecture, rink and window displays as installed. Do not promise the lit Rockefeller tree: the 2026 lighting date is not yet published.

10:30–12:00

Bryant Park holiday browse

Winter Village is expected back fall 2026; recheck final opening dates. Choose one ornament for this trip and a warm drink. Watching the rink is easier than skating with a toddler.

12:00–14:30

Lunch + hotel nap

Keep the nap near your base. A big Black Friday shopping mission is not the goal.

15:00–16:30

Optional Rockettes matinee

2026 season begins Nov 4. Choose a confirmed Friday showtime near this block; runtime is approximately 90 minutes. Under 2 may sit on a parent’s lap without a ticket per the FAQ.

17:00–18:00

AperiBar or nearby dinner

Keep dinner near the hotel or Radio City. If you skip the show, replace it with a short Top of the Rock visit only if skies are clear; allow ~$100–$140 for two adults, verify checkout.

At the table

$220 family allowance: breakfast $35, market/lunch $65, dinner $90, snacks $30. AperiBar is convenient for LUMA; Jams is better for a northern Midtown base.

With Rue

Stroller for windows, hotel for nap, carrier for dense crowds. The show is optional: loud sound and sitting still may be a poor fit. Ask about stroller storage and leave gracefully if Rue is done.

How to get around

Northern Midtown → Rockefeller: 10–20 minutes walking; Rockefeller → Bryant Park: ~15 minutes. Walk back for nap or take a short ride; ~2–3 miles spread across the day.

If the weather or mood turns

Rain: shorten outdoor browsing, visit the public areas of Grand Central, then a café. Avoid stacking a museum and a paid show on the same day.

Before you go

Only book the show after matching nap timing, return policy and exact seat price. Do not plan around an unannounced tree lighting or Santaland appointment.
