01 / The decision

The easiest yes.
At the hardest price.

Moar Gut removes most of the compromises in the other finalists. The question is whether included infant care, full board, stronger room separation and a more complete resort justify the premium.

Our read

Choose Moar Gut when this five-night stop is meant to be the resort centerpiece—not merely a place to sleep between day trips.

It is the only finalist in this comparison that pairs an explicitly luxurious adult experience with care beginning in the first month of life, full-board food, a separate child bedroom, deep farm access and serious water facilities.

€937.50 more than Forsthofgut · €1,862 more than Habachklause
Pick it for

Baby-first luxury

Pick it for

True resort depth

The farm, Kinderhof, pools, riding, spa and mountain program can carry five active days without repetitive driving.

Pause if

Value outranks finish

03 / The physical experience

A resort with
real range.

Moar Gut’s selling point is not one hero feature. It is how the suite, water, farm, child spaces, dining and alpine setting reinforce one another.

04 / What is really included

The complete
Moar Gut read.

The core family day is covered unusually well. The meaningful extras are treatments, selected sports and riding, drinks outside the stated board, transfers and tax.

Suite reality

Bedtime gets a door—but not three full rooms.

The 40 m² Vergissmeinnicht is a two-room suite: adults sleep in the combined living/sleeping area, while the child room has two single beds.

  • Pine double bed and separate child room
  • Bathtub plus shower and separate WC
  • Air conditioning, terrace and Nespresso
  • Limited view; adult-room window faces stables
Baby care

Built for a one-year-old, not merely welcoming.

Supervised care begins at 30 days and runs daily from 9 a.m. to 9 p.m. The separate baby/toddler zone includes crawling space, changing facilities and a sound-separated nap room.

  • Register by the previous evening
  • Pine infant beds and sensory play
  • Strollers, carriers and back carriers available
  • Confirm guaranteed hours and session limits
Water + wellness

More ways to swim than you need.

The water story covers a 25 m heated outdoor pool, panorama indoor pool, 34°C baby pool, toddler water, a natural lake and a separate Nature Spa.

  • Main indoor and outdoor pools listed at 32°C
  • Pools and lake accessible from 7 a.m.
  • Sauna and spa generally 11 a.m.–7 p.m.
  • Baby floating and treatments cost extra
Farm + horses

This is more than a themed petting corner.

The family-run organic farm includes cows and calves, while the small-animal area adds donkeys, pigs, alpacas, goats, rabbits, chickens, geese and sheep.

  • Daily animal access
  • Choose an animal sponsorship during the stay
  • 17 Icelandic horses
  • Riding experiences begin from age two
Active days

Leave the rental car parked—or skip it entirely.

Guided hikes, the weekly Moar Gut Alm outing, the Berg-Gesund mountain program and the valley’s walking and cycling paths create active days from the resort.

  • Panoramic loop around the resort
  • Guided program included; some gear extra
  • Bikes and e-bikes available for a fee
  • Tennis, climbing and archery on site
What costs more

The extras are visible, but get quotes.

The €5,200 is a projected five-night room-and-board total from the previously checked nightly rate—not a refreshed checkout today.

  • €31.50 known local tax for two adults
  • Airport or station transfer
  • Riding, rentals and selected private activities
  • Spa, baby spa and beauty treatments

06 / How five nights work

Five active days.
No recovery day.

The point of staying five nights is to use the resort properly while still getting into the mountains. Arrival day begins immediately with farm, pool and dinner.

Night 01

Farm + first swim

Check in, meet the animals, walk the grounds and use the outdoor pool before the six-course dinner.

Day 02

Grossarl outdoors

Take a guided family hike or valley route, then return for lunch, cake and the natural lake.

Day 03

Care + adult spa

Reserve infant care the prior evening. Use the shared time for the Nature Spa, a longer hike or both.

Day 04

Horses + Kinderhof

Ask which age-two pony experience can include the child, then combine the farm with indoor play and pools.

Day 05

Alm or favorite repeat

Join the weekly Moar Gut Alm outing if it aligns—or repeat the best mountain, farm and water combination.

07 / Getting there without a car

Transfer in.
Stay put.

A five-night rental car is not necessary if this is a resort-focused stay. The trade-off is paying for a prearranged transfer and surrendering spontaneous regional day trips.

Best no-car plan

Train to St. Johann, hotel shuttle to the door.

St. Johann im Pongau is about 16 km away and is the hotel’s normal rail gateway. Ask Moar Gut to quote a private pickup with an infant car seat.

  • Book pickup before arrival
  • Infant seats available by advance request
  • Resort and local walking days work car-free
  • Compare transfer cost with five-day rental + fuel
From the airports

Salzburg is materially easier than Munich.

The hotel describes Salzburg Airport as about one hour away and Munich as roughly 2.5–3 hours by road. It arranges private transfers from either.

  • Salzburg Airport: about 70 km / one hour
  • Munich Airport: roughly 2.5–3 hours
  • Prices are quote-only
  • Door-to-door transfer avoids car-seat logistics
When a car earns its keep

Only if the stay becomes an excursion base.

Rent one if several days will be spent on independent lake, waterfall or cross-valley outings. Do not rent it merely for the arrival and departure drives.

  • Useful for flexible weather pivots
  • Useful for touring onward to another resort
  • Less useful for a farm–pool–spa stay
  • Free garage is available at the resort
Ask for two numbers

Price the transfer both ways.

Request the exact private-transfer cost for the whole family from Salzburg Airport and St. Johann station, including the infant seat and all fees.

  • Airport pickup and departure timing
  • Station pickup and rail connection buffer
  • Cancellation rules
  • Whether local resort shuttles are included

08 / Before paying

Ask these seven questions.

  1. What is the exact five-night total for the September dates, including the one-year-old, all taxes and every mandatory fee?
  2. Is the quoted Vergissmeinnicht the exact pictured configuration, and how prominent are the stable-facing window and limited terrace view?
  3. Can infant-care sessions be reserved before arrival, is care guaranteed after prior-evening registration, and are there daily limits?
  4. Which drinks are included for adults and children under the exact gourmet full-board rate?
  5. Which animal, pony, Alm and guided-hike activities will operate on these five specific days, and which cost extra?
  6. What is the family transfer price from Salzburg Airport, Munich Airport and St. Johann station with an infant seat?
  7. Are the outdoor pool and natural swimming lake normally fully available in mid-September?
