Availability correction

The other five sub-€4k categories are not real choices.

The unfiltered engine shows categories that fit the occupancy but are not bookable for these dates. With Only available rooms enabled, the shortlist is exactly Glück, Heimatgefühl and Bergbalance. This page now excludes every unavailable room from its rankings and galleries.

“Views” vs “direct views”

Yes—Bergbalance is making the stronger claim.

Bergbalance“Direct views of the Leogang mountains” and a balcony. This most likely signals a more front-facing, immediately visible mountain orientation.

Glück“Views of the Leogang mountains,” from a balcony or terrace. The wording allows a more angled, partial or location-dependent view.

HeimatgefühlThe full details mention a balcony with mountain views, but the three headline bullets do not sell the view at all.

The caveat: Forsthofgut does not publish a formal definition of “direct,” an angle, or an unobstructed-view guarantee—and Bergbalance layouts can vary by location. Treat it as the strongest view signal, then ask the hotel to confirm the assigned unit’s orientation in writing.

05 / Fine print

What this quote does—and does not—lock in.

Occupancy

Two adults and one child age one. Changing the child’s age or adding another child can change both eligible rooms and package pricing.

Cancellation

The engine states: free until three months before arrival; 40% thereafter until one month; 70% until one week; 90% during arrival week. Confirm the final rate’s conditions before paying.

USD

Dollar figures are reference conversions, not card charges. Your bank’s exchange rate and foreign-transaction fee will control the actual total.
