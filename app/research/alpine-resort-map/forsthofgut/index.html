01 / The decision

It wins on desire.
Not on logistics.

Forsthofgut is the resort you are more likely to remember as a special hotel. Habachklause is the resort more likely to make every hour with a one-year-old easier.

Our read

Choose Forsthofgut if the adults will actually use the spa, value refined meals and design, and want the Asitz lift almost outside the door.

The main compromise is not the €924.50 five-night price gap alone. It is paying that premium while sharing one 30 m² sleep space, buying adult lunch separately, and arranging paid care for the one-year-old.

€924.50 more than Habachklause before lunch or babysitting
Pick it for

A real adults’ hotel

The larger, more layered spa and stronger design language make resort time feel like a holiday for the parents too.

Pick it for

Lift-and-hike access

The Asitzbahn is roughly 200 metres away, and the included regional card covers mountain rides and useful local transport.

Pause if

Sleep separation matters

The priced Berglust room has no separate child bedroom. Confirm whether a family suite with a closing door changes the economics.

03 / The physical experience

Why it feels
like the splurge.

The resort’s strongest argument is the combination of serious architecture, a mountain backdrop and a family zone that does not consume the adults-only experience.

04 / What is really included

The complete
Forsthofgut read.

Most of the headline resort infrastructure is included. The meaningful gaps for this family are adult lunch, standard care for a one-year-old, riding instruction and spa treatments.

Room reality

Berglust is lovely—but not a family suite.

The priced category is a 30 m² nature room for two or three people. It has oak flooring, balcony, box-spring bed, bathtub with integrated shower, separate WC, coffee machine and kettle.

  • A crib shares the adult sleep space
  • No climate control
  • Layouts, colorways and orientation vary
  • Some rooms have restricted views
Family infrastructure

Excellent shared play; age two is the threshold.

Rocky’s Club runs daily from 9 a.m. to 9 p.m. for children two and older, with supervised lunch and dinner. A 24-hour parent-child room works for a one-year-old but is unsupervised.

  • 170 m² kids’ club
  • Indoor Ninja Parkour and art studio
  • Baby package and loaner stroller/carrier
  • Babysitter can be arranged for a fee
Water + wellness

The best adult spa of the three finalists.

The 5,700 m² waldSPA separates family energy from adult quiet. Family facilities include a 21 m indoor pool, baby and child pools, water workshop, 70 m timed slide and family saunas.

  • Family pool open 24 hours
  • Baby pool 25 cm deep; child pool 50 cm
  • Both toddler pools heated to 33°C
  • Adults-only pools, saunas and relaxation spaces
Farm + riding

Beautifully programmed, less work-farm immersive.

miniGUT brings sheep, rabbits, ponies, pigs, goats, donkeys and alpacas together beside a wildlife enclosure. The new riding complex adds a serious paid horse program.

  • Weekly wildlife feeding with the host family
  • 20 × 40 m indoor riding arena
  • 30,000 m² pasture
  • Paid riding, horse walks and pony walks
Mountain days

Leave the car parked.

The Asitzbahn is roughly 200 m away. The included Saalfelden Leogang Card opens lift rides, regional transport and local attractions; five guided hikes run weekly.

  • Two daily mountain rides listed with the summer card
  • Family trails and Senses Experience Park
  • Free hiking poles and backpacks
  • Guest Mobility Ticket across SalzburgerLand
What costs more

Budget beyond the room total.

The five-night projection covers the compared room and ForsthofgutCUISINE. It does not establish a final five-night checkout total or cover every resort experience.

  • Adult lunch and staffed-bar drinks
  • Babysitter for the one-year-old
  • Riding lessons and guided pony walks
  • Spa treatments and visitor tax

What sounds especially good

Three menus, not one compromise menu.

The official concept treats vegan dishes as a complete line from breakfast through dinner, while R50 makes local sourcing visible. That is a better adult dining story than a standard family buffet.

Booking question

Confirm how à-la-carte restaurants interact with board.

Forsthofgut also operates Noon, silva, 1617 and Mizūmi. Do not assume those meals are included or credited against the main-restaurant board; ask before planning a restaurant rotation.

06 / How five nights work

Enough time to use
what you paid for.

Five nights suit Forsthofgut better than a fast stop. The key is protecting resort time instead of leaving for a long excursion every morning.

Night 01

Farm + family pool

Use miniGUT and the 24-hour indoor pool immediately. Dinner is already the evening event.

Day 02

Asitz mountain

Walk to the lift for the Senses Experience Park and an easy family trail; return for the afternoon snack.

Day 03

Resort day

Family spa, parent-child room and a prebooked paid babysitter so the adults can use the quiet spa together.

Day 04

Guided outdoors

Match the hotel’s guided-hike program to energy and weather; keep a pony walk as the bookable add-on.

Day 05

Choose the favorite

Repeat the lift, take a short local outing, or do nothing beyond farm, pool and a long dinner.

07 / Before paying

Ask these six questions.

  1. What is the exact five-night total for September 11–16, including the one-year-old and visitor tax?
  2. Can Berglust be guaranteed with the best view and enough crib clearance—and which upgrade gives a closing bedroom door?
  3. What does a babysitter cost, what hours can be reserved, and can five-night guests prebook before arrival?
  4. Are any adult lunch credits included with the quoted package, and how are silva, 1617 and Mizūmi charged?
  5. Which horse experiences allow a one-year-old as an accompanied observer or participant, and what is running during the stay?
  6. Which Saalfelden Leogang Card benefits and lift schedules remain active for the exact September dates?
