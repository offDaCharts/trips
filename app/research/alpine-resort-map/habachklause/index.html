01 / The decision

It wins the trip
you are taking now.

For a five-night stay with a one-year-old, Habachklause packages the most consequential conveniences into the quoted product: a door between sleep spaces, baby care and food all day.

Our read

Choose Habachklause if the room must keep functioning after bedtime, both adults want predictable time together, and you would rather know the food budget now.

It costs €924.50 less in the five-night comparison before accounting for Forsthofgut’s adult lunches or babysitter. Its spa is less spectacular, but the family infrastructure is more complete for this exact child age.

The pragmatic winner for two adults + one-year-old
Pick it for

Separate sleep zones

The compared Edelweiß suite has a master bedroom, children’s room and living room—an enormous difference after a one-year-old’s bedtime.

Pick it for

Included baby care

The Baby Club accepts ages 0–2.9 and operates daily; sessions require registration by 6 p.m. the previous evening.

Pause if

The hotel must wow the adults

Forsthofgut has the stronger architectural, spa and destination-dining pull. Habachklause sells ease and warmth more than design drama.

03 / The physical experience

The whole family
lives outside.

The hotel is smaller, but its outdoor grounds read immediately: water play, farm, lawns, climbing and vehicle tracks all sit around a compact base.

04 / What is really included

The complete
Habachklause read.

This is unusually close to a known trip cost. The important extras are alcohol or staff-ordered drinks, paid lessons and treatments—not basic family functioning.

Suite reality

The room solves bedtime.

The compared Edelweiß category is 40–45 m² on the second floor of the farmhouse chalet, with balcony, master bedroom, a separate children’s bedroom and another separate living room.

  • Fixed bunk in the child room
  • Crib makes the living room feel smaller
  • Shower and WC, not bathtub
  • No elevator; not barrier-free
Baby Club

Care begins at zero, not two.

Baby care for ages 0–2.9 is included within a published 73.5-hour weekly operation. The club runs Monday–Friday 8:30 a.m.–8 p.m. and weekends noon–8 p.m.

  • Register by 6 p.m. the previous evening
  • Dedicated baby club space
  • Kids’ Club begins at age three
  • Confirm session lengths and availability
Water + wellness

Smaller, but complete enough.

FamilyWellness includes a panoramic indoor pool, family cabanas, an indoor water playground with three small slides and a heated outdoor spring-water pool during the September 2026 stay.

  • Family sauna and steam facilities
  • Separate adults-only relaxation and sauna area
  • Outdoor whirlpool and yoga room
  • New lazy river is not until July 2027
Farm + outdoors

The farm is part of the daily rhythm.

Families can join daily feeding of ponies, goats, pigs, rabbits, guinea pigs, ducks and fish. Cows and calves live at the adjacent farm; free pony sessions run at set times.

  • Daily feeding with the animal keeper
  • Free pony rides 1–2 times weekly, Apr–Nov
  • Paid professional riding in summer
  • 4,000 m² outdoor play landscape
Excursion fit

Use the valleys, not the highway.

Habachtal’s emerald trail, the Wildkogel lift system and Krimml are the natural outside days. Free bikes, helmets and child seats make short valley movement easier.

  • Wildkogel adventure arena and family walks
  • Habachtal emerald exploration
  • Krimml waterfall day
  • Back carriers and buggies available to borrow
What costs more

The gaps are mostly optional.

The compared board includes food and self-service nonalcoholic drinks. Anything ordered from service staff or the Kristallbar is charged unless a VIP board applies.

  • Alcohol and staffed-bar drinks
  • Professional riding and children’s courses
  • Spa treatments and e-bike rental
  • Room requests, taxes and arrival/departure packages

What sounds especially useful

The child does not have to match restaurant time.

The HiPP bar runs 6 a.m.–10:30 p.m., while dinner also has a separate children’s buffet. Fresh bibs, high chairs or rockers and coloring materials are listed in the restaurant setup.

Optional upgrade

VIP board changes the proposition—and cost.

Designated VIP rooms add à-la-carte breakfast, up to seven-course dinner, many bar drinks and selected wine. Buying VIP board separately is listed at €99 per adult per night and must cover the entire stay and family.

06 / How five nights work

Five days without
family admin.

This resort can carry whole days by itself. The best version alternates two nearby outings with farm, pool and care time—not five consecutive drives.

Night 01

Animals + pool

Walk the compact grounds, find the feeding time and let the pools occupy the late afternoon before included dinner.

Day 02

Habachtal

Use the emerald trail as the signature valley outing, then return for cake instead of sourcing lunch and snacks outside.

Day 03

Parent time

Reserve the Baby Club the prior evening, then use the adults-only area or book a treatment while care is included.

Day 04

Wildkogel

Take the lift for the adventure arena and an easy mountain walk; use a back carrier where buggy routes stop.

Day 05

Farm or Krimml

Choose a true resort day with pony programming—or make Krimml the one larger excursion if weather is clear.

07 / Before paying

Ask these six questions.

  1. What is the exact five-night total for September 11–16, including all taxes and the one-year-old?
  2. Which exact Edelweiß suite is being assigned, and how many doors separate the crib, adult bedroom and living area?
  3. Can Baby Club sessions be reserved before arrival, are there daily time limits, and is care guaranteed once preregistered?
  4. Will the heated outdoor spring-water pool be fully operating throughout the exact September stay?
  5. Which animal feeding, free pony ride and paid lesson slots are scheduled during those five days?
  6. Are any room-location requests worth the €10 nightly request charge, especially given the second-floor/no-elevator caveat?
