The fast read

They are not five versions of the same thing.

Best overall fit

Forsthofgut

The strongest balance of beautiful design, serious spa, a real children’s farm, and easy access to Leogang’s mountains.

Best with a one-year-old

Moar Gut

Baby care starts at 30 days, full board is built in, and the whole resort is designed around family logistics—not adapted to them.

Best value with a live quote

Habachklause

€667.60 per night, non-alcoholic all-inclusive, daily baby care, farm life, and a much smaller 40-room scale.

Best grand-resort splurge

Stanglwirt

The most complete adult resort—12,000 m² wellness, Lipizzaners, golf and tennis—while still treating families seriously.

Read before booking

A quote is only exact when the booking engine says it is.

01

Every total is normalized by its actual nights.

The original checks covered four nights: Sep 11–15 for Forsthofgut and Habachklause, Sep 13–17 for Moar Gut, and a four-night Stanglwirt package within Sep 13–18. Each nightly benchmark now projects a five-night stay; the displayed totals are planning figures, not refreshed checkout quotes.

02

“Published” is not the same as checkout-ready.

DAS EDELWEISS’s €923.75 nightly figure comes from its published FamilyMOMENTS floor, not a date-locked family checkout. Its €4,618.75 five-night total is a projection; the other resorts are projected from previously checked checkout or package rates.

03

Meals change the value comparison.

Moar Gut and Habachklause cover the full day. Forsthofgut includes breakfast, afternoon food and dinner. DAS EDELWEISS is half board. Stanglwirt includes breakfast only; five hotel dinners add €770.

Nightly price and inclusion evidence was checked August 1, 2026 for two adults and one child age one, then extended mathematically to five nights on August 2 without re-running availability. Prices are in euros and can change until booked. Five-night totals are planning projections; original quote dates, source links and caveats are inside each modal.
