The overview

Only two are neighbors.

Moar Gut and DAS EDELWEISS sit about 3.3 km apart in Großarl. Forsthofgut, Habachklause, and Stanglwirt each belong to a different valley or resort orbit, so mountain-road drive times will be much longer than straight-line distances suggest.
