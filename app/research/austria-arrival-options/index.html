The real choice

One city day or two lake days.

Paris is cheaper in airfare and avoids the red-eye, but adds two hotel nights and reaches Forsthofgut around dinner. Austria is more expensive in airfare and begins tired, but gives you a gentle lake afternoon plus one strong mountain-lake day.
