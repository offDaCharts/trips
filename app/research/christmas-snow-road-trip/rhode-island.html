King bedroom, separate living area with sleeper sofa, kitchenette and washer/dryer. Free Pack ’n Play by request, subject to availability. Elevators serve all floors.

The tradeoff

More casual than Ocean House. The beach is across the road, and some suites face the pond. Rooftop hot tub is for adult downtime; outdoor access is weather-dependent.

The quick take

Mill Street for charm.
Newport Beach for easy family days.

Both are worth pricing before committing to Stone House. Weekapaug belongs on the list if the Ocean House atmosphere matters more than the lowest total. Fly ATL → Providence (PVD), then rent a car; start by comparing a three-night stay.

White Christmas reality & weather sources

NOAA 1991–2020 daily normals averaged across December 24–31: Newport State Airport about 41°/27°F, Westerly State Airport about 42°/28°F. These are climate averages, not a forecast for your dates.

Nearby station histories put a white Christmas at 17.5% in Tiverton and 21.3% in Kingston. That means at least one inch of natural snow on the ground December 25, not necessarily falling snow. These are regional context, not hotel odds. Utah or Vermont remains the stronger snow choice.
