If a white Christmas is the point

Put the snow odds first.

Dogwood Den / Beech Mountain leads this shortlist. For a stronger snow bet, keep Snowshoe, WV in play: about 60% historical odds of at least 1 inch on the ground on Christmas Day. A specific stay accepting both dogs still needs choosing.

Revisit the Snowshoe idea ↗
Snow on the ground ≠ snow falling

These are climate averages, not a Christmas 2026 forecast. Even Snowshoe is no guarantee. Ski-area snowmaking can help with snow activities, but does not make the cabin yard white.

Weather sources & how to use them

Temperatures average NOAA’s 1991–2020 daily normals for December 24–31, rounded to °F. Stations represent the area, not each property. Snowshoe’s corresponding high / low is 32° / 18°F.

Christmas percentages use natural snow depth ≥1 inch on December 25: Snowshoe 59.5%, Townsend 5S 6.3%, Asheville airport 6.3%. NOAA marks Banner Elk, Beech Mountain and Hot Springs as insufficient data for this statistic; we have not invented percentages. Beech’s ranking is our elevation/climate-based assessment.

For this snow goal, favor late December over Thanksgiving. Check the forecast and snow reports in the final 7–10 days, then again 2–3 days out; choose cancellation terms that leave room to adapt.
