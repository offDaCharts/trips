Snow, and the choice I’d make

Green Mountain for charm.
Von Trapp for the setting.

I’d price those two first. Stoweflake is worth checking if the suite budget needs to stay lower; Spruce Peak makes sense if the mountain-base resort experience is worth a substantial premium.

How much snow confidence should you have?

NOAA’s historical white-Christmas data in the existing research puts Burlington Airport at 62.3%, versus 21.3% at Kingston, Rhode Island. These are regional reference stations, not odds for a Stowe hotel. Mount Mansfield summit is much higher than any of these stays, so its 99% figure should not be used for the resorts.

“White Christmas” means at least one inch of natural snow on the ground on December 25, not snow falling that day. It does not establish New Year’s conditions. Higher elevations generally help, but a thaw can affect every option.
