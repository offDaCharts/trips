What the weather numbers mean

Temperatures average NOAA 1991–2020 daily normals for December 24–31, rounded to °F. Park City town is lower than Stein Eriksen Lodge; Morrisville–Stowe airport represents the valley, not the slopes; Westerly airport represents coastal Rhode Island.

A white Christmas means at least 1 inch of natural snow on the ground December 25, not snow falling that day. NOAA’s station statistics: Kingston RI 21.3%, Burlington VT 62.3%, Mount Mansfield summit VT 99%. These are regional context, not hotel-specific percentages. Our Utah/Vermont ranking is a climate-and-elevation-based assessment. No location guarantees snow in 2026.

Late December is the stronger snow choice than Thanksgiving. Recheck forecasts, webcams and snow cover 7–10 days out and again 2–3 days before travel. Strict holiday cancellation terms can limit changes.
