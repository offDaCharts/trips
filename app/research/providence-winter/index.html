Work setup and Rue’s daily rhythm

Choose a real bedroom door, space for a travel crib, and a table or desk away from nap space. Ask for a recent Wi-Fi speed test and a photo of the chair; “dedicated workspace” is not proof of comfort for a full week. The two-bedroom rentals offer the most flexibility, but the actual crib setup still needs confirmation. Thames St Guesthouse and the Middletown guesthouse list Pack ’n Plays.

Keep most weekday outings within 15–30 minutes, with a longer trip only after an early finish. Plan warm indoor breaks and time for Rue to move around. Use a carrier for crowded historic interiors, with a stroller for town sidewalks when clear. The default plan does not assume Rue will skate or ride ice bumper cars.

Lights and cold air, with snow a bonus

December Providence averages are roughly 43°F by day and 28°F overnight. Early December snow cover is unreliable; this is a good Christmas-atmosphere trip, not a dependable snow-play trip.

Bring warm layers, rain protection, gloves and stroller warmth. Keep outdoor stops short if the harbor wind is sharp.
