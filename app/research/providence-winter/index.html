Saturday is the anchor festival day. Return to ATL Sunday; a later flight could allow another brief outing, but keep the airport buffer comfortable.

Work setup and Rue’s daily rhythm

Choose a real bedroom door, space for a travel crib, and a table or desk away from nap space. Ask for a recent Wi-Fi speed test and a photo of the chair; “dedicated workspace” is not proof of comfort for a full week. The two-bedroom rentals offer the most flexibility, but neither crib setup is verified.

Keep most weekday outings within 15–30 minutes, with a longer trip only after an early finish. Plan warm indoor breaks and time for Rue to move around. Use a carrier for crowded historic interiors, with a stroller for town sidewalks when clear. The default plan does not assume Rue will skate or ride ice bumper cars.

Flights, weather & a rough budget.

Fly Delta into PVD

ATL–PVD is the direct route to price first. Two paid adult seats + Rue as a lap infant, following your usual setup. Flight times and fares are not yet quoted.

Rent a car for the town outings; prioritize included parking at the stay. Estimate 20–30 minutes PVD→Providence, 35–45 to Bristol, and 40–55 to Newport.

Lights and cold air, with snow a bonus

December Providence averages are roughly 43°F by day and 28°F overnight. Early December snow cover is unreliable; this is a good Christmas-atmosphere trip, not a dependable snow-play trip.

Bring warm layers, rain protection, gloves and stroller warmth. Keep outdoor stops short if the harbor wind is sharp.
