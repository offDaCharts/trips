Option 02 · Edinburgh to the Cairngorms

Scotland, built around the Highlands.

One energetic Edinburgh day, then loch islands, a real summit, Glencoe's mountain walls and two slower days in the Cairngorms.

Shape5 days / 5 nights
Bases3 practical bases
Best forMountain drama
Baby balanceCarrier + easy lochs

Why choose it

The option that feels most like the trip has already reached the Alps.

If the first leg should be a destination—not merely a convenient stop—Scotland wins. The progression from Edinburgh's volcanic ridge to Glencoe and the Cairngorms has real scale.

This deliberately skips Skye. In five nights, forcing Skye adds road hours and one-night hops that work against the family rhythm. Glencoe and the Cairngorms deliver the Highlands without turning the route into windshield tourism.

The sell

The strongest photos, most satisfying hikes and clearest match for a family that actively wants mountains, lakes and viewpoints.

The tradeoff

Wind and rain have more power here. The route needs flexible day order and a willingness to take the short version.

The route

Edinburgh → Glencoe → Cairngorms

September reality check

Let the clearest day choose the summit.

Conic Hill and Glencoe are exposed; wind matters as much as rain with a child in a carrier. Keep the summit day movable and use Glencoe Lochan, Loch an Eilein or Loch Morlich when the tops disappear. Glenmore Forest Park reopened in August 2026 after wildfire closures, but check the official status immediately before travel.

Gateway logic

Edinburgh keeps the route clean.

Fly into Edinburgh, collect the car once and leave from Edinburgh if the onward fare works—or compare a final-night Glasgow reposition.

Edinburgh is the better route start because Arthur's Seat turns the arrival day into a real outing. Returning there from Aviemore is about a half-day movement; schedule the onward Europe flight late enough or sleep near the airport. Glasgow is closer to Loch Lomond and Glencoe but less elegant for this eastward finish. This page does not quote live airfare.
