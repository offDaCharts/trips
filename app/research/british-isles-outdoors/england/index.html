Option 01 · London edge to the Jurassic Coast

England, outside the city.

Use London only as the gateway. The actual trip is royal parkland, chalk cliffs, New Forest ponies and two days on Dorset's sculpted coast.

Shape5 days / 5 nights
Bases3 practical bases

Why choose it

The broadest scenery, with the least flight friction.

This is the safest operational choice: Heathrow or Gatwick on one end, a dense road network, and plenty of ways to shorten a day without losing it.

It is not a miniature Scotland. The appeal is variety—deer meadow at breakfast, brilliant white cliffs the next day, ponies in open heath and then the Jurassic Coast. London can be a dinner and riverside walk, not the center of the vacation.

The sell

Highest odds that every day produces something memorable even if wind, rain or nap timing changes the plan.

The tradeoff

No true mountain day, and the Richmond-to-South-Downs transfer is vulnerable to London traffic.

The route

London's edge → Sussex → Dorset

Three bases: Richmond/London edge (1), Lewes or Alfriston (2), then Purbeck (2). The dashed line shows the planning sequence, not turn-by-turn navigation.

September reality check

Cliffs and animals change the rules.

Richmond's deer rut runs from September into autumn: give deer at least 50 metres and much more around an active male. In the New Forest, do not feed or pet ponies. On every coast day, stay well back from unguarded cliff edges and use the carrier rather than the stroller on uneven ground.

Gateway logic

Pick the London airport around the airfare.

Gatwick is the cleaner start for Sussex; Heathrow is better for Richmond and often has broader onward-Europe choice.

A one-way car from the arrival airport keeps this route simple. If the onward flight to Munich, Vienna or Zurich is materially better from Heathrow, finish Purbeck the prior afternoon and sleep near the airport; do not gamble on a same-morning Dorset drive. This page does not quote live airfare.
