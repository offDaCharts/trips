Option 03 · Dublin coast to County Clare

Ireland, from one wild coast to the other.

Touch Dublin lightly. The real story is Howth's headland, Glendalough's mountain lakes, the Sally Gap road and three nights among the Burren and Atlantic cliffs.

Why choose it

The most atmospheric option—and the one with the best bad-weather personality.

Ireland's advantage is not one giant objective. It is the sequence: harbor and gorse, a monastic lake valley, peat-dark uplands, limestone pavement and the open Atlantic.

The hikes are easy to scale, Doolin makes a sociable three-night base, and rain does not erase the mood. The compromise is one cross-island day. Build stops into it and it becomes a transfer with scenery, not dead time.

The sell

The strongest mixture of outdoors and sense of place: dramatic coast, distinctive geology, music, villages and a route that still feels good under clouds.

The tradeoff

Day three is a real driving day, and left-side driving on narrow rural roads demands more attention than the mileage suggests.

The route

Howth → Wicklow → County Clare

Three bases: Howth (1), Wicklow/Glendalough (1), then Doolin or north Clare (3). The dashed line shows the sequence rather than exact road navigation.

Current trail reality

Use the safe versions, not the heroic ones.

Glendalough trail work in 2026 affects the White Route/Spinc area, so this plan uses the Upper Lake, Green Road and Poulanass options instead. At the Cliffs of Moher, sections of the long coastal walk remain closed; stay on official designated paths and use the paved visitor-centre viewpoints in high wind.

Gateway logic

Dublin is the obvious in-and-out.

Dublin Airport makes Howth a useful first day and provides the broadest onward-Europe choice. Shannon can shorten the road trip, but it usually narrows the onward flight menu.

For a five-night one-way route, price DUB-in/SNN-out only if the onward mainland flight works exceptionally well. Otherwise keep the car through County Clare, then drive roughly three hours to Dublin and place a final airport night before a morning flight. This page does not quote live airfare.
