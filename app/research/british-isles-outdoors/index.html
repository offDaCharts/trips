Trip field notes
England Scotland Ireland

First-leg shortlist · five days outdoors

Three ways into the wild.

England for coast and easy airport logic. Scotland for the biggest mountain drama. Ireland for wild Atlantic atmosphere. Each route is paced for two adults and a one-year-old—active days, useful arrival time, and no ceremonial recovery day.

The quick answer

Same brief. Three very different moods.

For this family and an onward Austria leg, Scotland is the most exciting outdoor match. England is the lowest-friction choice. Ireland may be the most atmospheric, but it creates one longer cross-country driving day.

London → South Downs → Dorset

England

One useful London-edge day, then chalk cliffs, free-roaming New Forest ponies and the Jurassic Coast.

  • Best airport and onward-flight flexibility
  • Most varied scenery with gentler fallback walks
  • Less alpine; southern traffic can blunt the rhythm
See the England route

Edinburgh → Trossachs → Cairngorms

Scotland

A brief Edinburgh taste followed by lochs, a real summit, Glencoe and family-friendly Highland forest.

  • Strongest scenery and closest match to Austria
  • Excellent carrier hikes plus easy loch loops
  • More weather exposure and the longest scenic drives
See the Scotland route

Howth → Wicklow → County Clare

Ireland

Dublin gets a single evening; the real trip is cliff paths, Glendalough, peatland roads and Burren limestone.

  • Best combination of wild coast and local character
  • Good short-walk menu for unpredictable weather
  • One purposeful cross-island transfer day
See the Ireland route
