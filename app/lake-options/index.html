01 / The short answer

Distance changes the decision.

Hintersteiner is the easy local lake. Achensee is the large-scale scenery option that still makes sense as a day trip. Piburger and Obernberger are purposeful excursions. Plansee and Lünersee are striking—but they point decisively away from Forsthofgut.

Corrected route check

The shared research estimated Plansee at 1 hr 20–1 hr 50. Current road routing from Höhenleitweg 4 is closer to 2 hr 31 one way, before traffic or stops.

03 / Visual comparison

Choose the kind of lake day.

Doable anytime

Hintersteiner See

Clear turquoise water directly beneath the Wilder Kaiser. Best for a short shore walk, photographs and an outing that does not consume the day.

  • From Going: 11 min · 10 km
  • Onward to Forsthofgut: 50 min · 44 km
  • With Rue: easy short version; the full 5.2 km loop includes rougher sections
Directions ↗
Best destination lake

Achensee

A vast, fjord-like lake between the Karwendel and Rofan ranges. Pertisau gives you promenades, boats and a dramatic wall of mountains without requiring a hard hike.

  • From Going: 57 min · 60 km
  • Onward to Forsthofgut: 1 hr 36 · 94 km
  • With Rue: promenade and boat are the easiest high-reward combination
Directions ↗
Dedicated outing

Piburger See

Smaller, calmer and more intimate than Achensee. The reward is a forest-ringed mirror lake above Oetz; the tradeoff is a long westward drive and an approach walk.

  • From Going: 1 hr 54 · 139 km
  • Onward to Forsthofgut: 2 hr 33 · 172 km
  • With Rue: carrier is more flexible than stroller around the lake
Directions ↗
Dedicated outing

Obernberger See

The most storybook composition: a high lake, forest and Maria am See chapel under the Tribulaun. Unlike roadside lakes, it requires roughly a 30-minute uphill walk from parking.

  • From Going: 1 hr 41 · 117 km
  • Onward to Forsthofgut: 2 hr 21 · 151 km
  • With Rue: bring the carrier; not a quick pull-off
Directions ↗
Wrong direction tomorrow

Plansee

A long, blue-green mountain lake with roadside viewpoints and easy scenic access. It earns the praise from the shared research—but not as a stop on the way to Leogang.

  • From Going: 2 hr 31 · 181 km
  • Onward to Forsthofgut: 3 hr 11 · 215 km
  • Verdict: save for a Reutte / Füssen itinerary
Directions ↗
Future trip

Lünersee

The most genuinely high-alpine option, reached by cable car above Brand. It is spectacular, but lift schedules, mountain weather and six-plus hours of driving make it a separate expedition.

  • From Going: 3 hr 09 · 234 km
  • Onward to Forsthofgut: 3 hr 47 · 268 km
  • Verdict: pair with Vorarlberg, not this stay
