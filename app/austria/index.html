What this is

A research layer,
not a route.

The video jumps across the country for visual storytelling; it is not a sensible point-to-point itinerary. The map preserves that distinction: numbered pins follow the video, but there is deliberately no route line between them.

Each entry pairs a frame from the video with the creator’s main takeaway, the real location, the kind of experience it is, and the practical caveat most worth knowing.

!

Important 2026 update

The Silvretta High Alpine Road shown in the video is closed.

The official operator says the through-road will remain closed to public traffic until summer 2030 following rockfalls and mudslides. The Vermuntbahn and selected Bielerhöhe experiences can still operate separately—check live conditions.

Read before planning

Three useful distinctions.

01

Some “places” are full regions.

Vorarlberg, Wachau, and Lake Constance are broad areas. Their pins are orientation points, not a single attraction entrance.

02

Two highlights are serious objectives.

The Donnerkogel Sky Ladder is an exposed via ferrata; Olpererhütte is a steep mountain hike. Neither belongs in a casual sightseeing bucket.

03

High-alpine access is seasonal.

Glacier lifts, cog railways, mountain huts, panoramic roads, and cable cars need same-season schedule and weather checks.
