The details behind the shortlist.

Snow odds: historical snow on the ground, not a forecast

New Hampshire uses nearby North Conway’s 76.3%; Rangeley uses 93.8%. Both measure at least one inch of natural snow on the ground on December 25, using NOAA’s 1991–2020 climate normals. These are regional references, not measurements at the houses. NOAA Christmas snow statistics ↗

Québec City’s 92% uses 2000–2024 and at least two centimeters on the ground at 7 a.m. on December 25. Its threshold and period differ from the U.S. data. None of these figures guarantees a white Christmas in 2026 or predicts snow falling that day. Environment Canada ↗

Flights, drives and winter arrival

Portland Jetport’s published December 2026 schedule includes Delta’s Atlanta nonstop. Flight time is roughly 2½–2¾ hours. December route schedule · Flight duration.

Québec City (YQB) requires a connection from Atlanta. Delta flies nonstop to Montréal (YUL), roughly 2¾ hours, but that adds about 3–3½ hours of driving to Stoneham. YQB connections · Delta to Montréal.

Drives are planning estimates in normal conditions, not exact cabin routes. Add time for winter weather, stops and rental pickup. NØRR and RidgeView specifically require AWD/4x4 and winter tires. Exact holiday flights and fares have not been checked.
