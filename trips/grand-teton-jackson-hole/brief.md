# Grand Teton + Jackson Hole

Current concept: Jackson arrival, Grand Teton base, possible light Yellowstone edge only if it does not overload the route.

Why it fits: domestic mountain scenery, iconic lakes, wildlife, cabins, and short hikes with easier logistics than Alaska.

Early stay ideas:

- Jenny Lake Lodge: https://www.gtlc.com/lodges/jenny-lake-lodge
- Jackson Lake Lodge: https://www.gtlc.com/lodges/jackson-lake-lodge
- Spring Creek Ranch: https://www.springcreekranch.com/
- Signal Mountain Lodge: https://www.signalmountainlodge.com/

Anchor activities:

- NPS Grand Teton hiking: https://www.nps.gov/grte/planyourvisit/hike.htm
- Grand Teton Lodge activities: https://www.gtlc.com/

Watchouts:

- Farm-to-table/farmstay fit is weaker.
- Jackson lodging is expensive.
- September mornings can be cold.
