# Alaska Kenai + Anchorage

Current concept: Anchorage, Girdwood, Seward/Kenai Fjords, and Palmer/Matanuska, avoiding an overloaded Denali plus Kenai plus remote-lodge route.

Why it fits: glaciers, wildlife, coastal scenery, lodges, and big once-in-a-while landscapes.

Early stay ideas:

- Alaska Glacier Lodge: https://www.akglacierlodge.com/
- Kenai Fjords Glacier Lodge: https://www.alaskawildland.com/lodges/kenai-fjords-glacier-lodge/
- Stillpoint Lodge: https://stillpointlodge.com/
- Within the Wild: https://withinthewild.com/

Anchor activities:

- Alaska itineraries for kids under 5: https://www.alaska.org/trip-ideas/itineraries-for-families-with-kids-under-5
- Alaska Wildlife Conservation Center: https://alaskawildlife.org/
- Alaska family vacations: https://www.alaskatravel.com/vacations/family/

Watchouts:

- Seasonal operations start tapering in September.
- Boat tours may be too long or rough with a one-year-old.
- Luxury lodges can push the budget up fast.
