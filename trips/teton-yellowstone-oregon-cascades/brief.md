# Teton + Yellowstone + Oregon Cascades

Current concept: an expanded alternate version of the Grand Teton trip that keeps the Jackson/Teton magic but turns it into a broader western multi-destination route: Tetons, Yellowstone, then a flight hop to Oregon for Bend, Crater Lake, Mount Hood, and the Columbia River Gorge.

Why it fits: still domestic and mountain-forward, but it solves the original Teton plan's narrowness by adding geothermal Yellowstone drama and a totally different second act in Oregon: volcanic lakes, high desert, waterfalls, food carts, and easier soft-landing services near Portland.

Working flight shape:

- Option A: ATL -> Jackson Hole, drive Tetons/Yellowstone, fly JAC or BOI -> PDX/RDM, return PDX -> ATL.
- Option B: reverse it: ATL -> PDX, Oregon first, fly PDX/RDM -> JAC, return JAC -> ATL.
- Recommended default: start in Jackson because Yellowstone/Teton lodging is more date-sensitive and it is psychologically nicer to end with Oregon's Bend/Mount Hood/Portland services before the long flight home.

Anchor activities:

- Grand Teton easy lake days: Jenny Lake boat, String Lake picnic, Oxbow Bend wildlife pullouts.
- Yellowstone sampler: Old Faithful, Upper Geyser Basin boardwalks, Grand Prismatic overlook decision, West Thumb, Hayden Valley, Grand Canyon of the Yellowstone.
- Oregon Cascades: Smith Rock viewpoint walk, High Desert Museum, Newberry Volcanic National Monument, Crater Lake Rim Drive, Toketee Falls, Mount Hood / Trillium Lake, Multnomah Falls and the Columbia Gorge.

Watchouts:

- This is a multi-flight open-jaw trip, so the flight pricing needs a live booking pass.
- Yellowstone can become a long-driving maze; keep two park bases and do not try to see every geyser basin.
- Crater Lake rim weather can be smoky or cloudy in late summer/early September; build one buffer day.
- The route works best with one-year-old pacing if Oregon has a practical condo/resort base with laundry.
