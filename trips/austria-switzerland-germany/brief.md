# Austria + Alpine Borders

Current concept: Munich or Zurich arrival, then a focused alpine route through Tyrol, Lech/Arlberg, and either Bavaria or eastern Switzerland/Appenzell.

Why it fits: closest to the Switzerland trip vibe without repeating Zermatt or Interlaken. Strongest candidate for alpine farms, animals, polished wellness, mountain lifts, and farm-to-table food.

Early stay ideas:

- Bio-Hotel Stanglwirt: https://www.stanglwirt.com/en/
- Rote Wand Gourmet Hotel: https://www.rotewand.com/en/
- Hotel Krallerhof: https://www.krallerhof.com/en/
- Farm Holidays Austria: https://www.farmholidays.com/en

Watchouts:

- True western Switzerland adds a lot of distance.
- Best resorts may need to be one- or two-night splurges.
- Route should stay disciplined with a baby.
