# Iceland Farm + South Coast

Current concept: Reykjavik, Golden Circle farm stay, compact South Coast, optional Snæfellsnes only if pacing allows.

Why it fits: farm stays, Icelandic horses, cows, waterfalls, geothermal pools, short scenic stops, and landscapes that feel very different from the Alps.

Early stay ideas:

- Efstidalur Farm Hotel: https://efstidalur.is/farm-hotel/
- Hotel Ranga: https://hotelranga.is/
- Visit Reykjavik: https://www.visitreykjavik.is/

Anchor activities:

- Efstidalur restaurant: https://efstidalur.is/restaurant/
- Secret Lagoon: https://secretlagoon.is/
- Golden Circle with kids: https://goldencircle.is/blog/golden-circle-with-kids

Watchouts:

- Blue Lagoon minimum age is two for entering the water: https://support.bluelagoon.com/hc/en-us
- Weather can be windy and wet.
- Full Ring Road is likely too much with a one-year-old.
