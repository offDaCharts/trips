# Banff + Lake Louise

Current concept: Calgary arrival, then Canmore/Banff plus two or three nights around Lake Louise.

Why it fits: huge mountain scenery, turquoise lakes, easy walks, stroller-friendly paths, lakefront splurge potential, and relatively simple logistics.

Early stay ideas:

- Fairmont Chateau Lake Louise: https://www.chateau-lake-louise.com/
- Post Hotel & Spa: https://posthotel.com/
- Moraine Lake Lodge: https://morainelake.com/

Anchor activities:

- Banff/Lake Louise kid-friendly activities: https://www.banfflakelouise.com/blog/kid-friendly-activities-Banff-Lake-Louise-Summer
- Lake Louise with kids: https://posthotel.com/blog/staying-in-lake-louise-with-kids-the-ultimate-all-season-guide

Watchouts:

- Less farmstead-forward than Austria or Iceland.
- Lake Louise and Moraine Lake lodging can get very expensive.
- Moraine Lake access requires shuttle planning.
