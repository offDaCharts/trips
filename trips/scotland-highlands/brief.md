# Scotland Highlands

Current concept: Edinburgh start, Perthshire/Trossachs, Cairngorms, and possibly Skye or the west coast if drive time stays reasonable.

Why it fits: storybook villages, farms, Highland animals, lochs, castles, estate activities, cozy food, and excellent indoor backup options.

Early stay ideas:

- Gleneagles: https://gleneagles.com/
- Monachyle Mhor: https://monachylemhor.net/
- The Fife Arms: https://thefifearms.com/

Anchor activities:

- Gleneagles family activities: https://gleneagles.com/pursuits/family-activities/
- Monachyle Mhor farm: https://monachylemhor.net/farm/
- VisitScotland Highlands for kids: https://www.visitscotland.com/things-to-do/family/highlands-for-kids

Watchouts:

- Rain is part of the experience.
- Skye can stretch the itinerary.
- Some luxury hotels may be more adult-culture than toddler-practical.
